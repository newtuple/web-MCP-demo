# Growth Operations Specialist

## About the Role

We are looking for a **Growth Operations Specialist** who will work directly with the founder to drive Newtuple's sales, marketing, and business development engine. This is a hands-on, execution-heavy role where you will own the full spectrum of go-to-market operations, from social media and content to GCC outreach and pipeline management.

This is not a pure marketing or pure sales role. You will be the connective tissue across everything GTM: publishing content, researching and reaching out to prospects, tracking pipeline, preparing collaterals, and coordinating across teams. You will operate as the founder's right hand on the commercial side of the business.

A non-negotiable requirement for this role is **fluency with AI tools**. You will be expected to use **Claude, Perplexity, ChatGPT**, and similar tools daily to research, draft, analyze, and automate. We are an AI company, and this role must reflect that. If you are not already heavily AI-augmented in how you work, this is not the right fit.

## Responsibilities

### Social Media & Content

* Plan, publish, and promote content across LinkedIn, X/Twitter, and other relevant channels.
* Track engagement metrics and optimize content strategy based on performance data.
* Create and maintain a consistent publishing cadence aligned with Newtuple's brand voice.

### GCC Research & Outreach

* Build and maintain target lists of prospects across segments and geographies.
* Research companies, decision-makers, and AI adoption signals to identify high-potential leads.
* Execute outbound outreach campaigns (email, LinkedIn) and manage follow-ups.

### Pipeline Management

* Maintain and track the sales pipeline, ensuring accurate and up-to-date records.
* Prepare weekly pipeline reports and flag deals that need attention.
* Coordinate with the founder on deal progression, follow-ups, and next steps.

### General GTM Support

* Prepare sales collaterals: pitch decks, one-pagers, case study summaries, and proposals.
* Coordinate meetings, demos, and events.
* Support any ad hoc sales, marketing, or business development initiatives as they come up.

## Requirements

### Must-Have

* **3-5 years of experience** in a growth, marketing, sales operations, or business development role.
* **Complete fluency with AI tools**: you must already be using Claude, Perplexity, ChatGPT, or similar tools daily in your workflow. We will ask you to demonstrate this.
* Strong written communication skills, the ability to draft compelling outreach, social posts, and collaterals quickly and independently.
* Experience with CRM tools, pipeline tracking, and basic reporting.
* Comfort working directly with a founder in a fast-paced, ambiguous environment where priorities shift.
* Self-starter who does not wait for detailed instructions; you see what needs to be done and do it.
* Familiarity with LinkedIn Sales Navigator, email outreach tools, and social media scheduling platforms.

### Good-to-Have

* Experience in B2B technology or AI/ML services companies.
* Background in targeting GCCs or enterprise accounts.
* Exposure to content marketing, SEO basics, or event coordination.
* Experience working in a startup or consulting environment.

## What We Offer

* Direct collaboration with the founder on strategy and execution.
* Exposure to cutting-edge AI projects across aviation, healthcare, finance, retail, and more.
* A fast-paced environment where your work has immediate, visible impact on company growth.
* Growth path into a leadership role as the GTM function scales.
* Flexible work culture, Pune-based.
