# **Job Description: Business Analyst – Generative AI**

## **About Newtuple Technologies**

At Newtuple Technologies, we are driving innovation at the intersection of business and cutting-edge technologies. With a strong focus on **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** and **Generative AI (GenAI)**, we build solutions that empower organizations to transform operations, unlock new opportunities, and deliver value at scale.

We are looking for a **Business Analyst (BA)** who can bridge business needs with AI-driven solutions, ensuring that our products and services align with evolving market demands and client expectations.

## **Role Overview**

As a **Business Analyst**, you will play a critical role in **gathering requirements, analyzing business processes, and designing AI-first solutions**. You will collaborate closely with product managers, data scientists, AI engineers, and stakeholders to define use cases and translate them into actionable product roadmaps.

A strong understanding of **AI and GenAI applications** will be essential in identifying opportunities where automation and intelligence can add measurable business value.

## **Key Responsibilities**

* **Requirement Analysis & Elicitation**

  * Work with stakeholders to capture, document, and validate business requirements.  
  * Translate business problems into AI/GenAI-driven use cases and product features.

* **Prompt engineering and Agent development**  
  * Work with business and technology teams in writing detailed prompts that will be deployed to AI applications  
  * Understand how to architect and deploy agents for business use cases  
  * Run evaluations against AI prompts & applications

    

* **AI/GenAI Solution Scoping**

  * Identify opportunities where AI (predictive models, NLP, computer vision) and GenAI (chatbots, copilots, content generation, automation) can be applied.  
  * Support feasibility studies by collaborating with data science and engineering teams.

* **Process & Gap Analysis**

  * Map existing workflows and propose AI-enhanced processes.  
  * Perform cost-benefit and ROI analysis for AI initiatives.

* **Stakeholder Communication**

  * Act as the liaison between technical teams and business units.  
  * Present insights, prototypes, and reports to stakeholders in a clear, data-driven manner.

* **Product Development Support**

  * Contribute to the creation of **PRDs, user stories, and acceptance criteria**.  
  * Support testing and validation of AI/GenAI models in real-world scenarios.

* **Market & Competitive Research**

  * Stay updated on AI and GenAI trends, tools (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face, LangChain), and competitor offerings.  
  * Recommend innovations that align with business strategy.

## **Required Skills & Qualifications**

* **Educational Background:** Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, Data Analytics, or a related field.

* **Experience:** 3–6 years as a Business Analyst or Product Analyst, preferably in **AI/ML-driven products**.

* **Knowledge of AI & GenAI:**

  * Familiarity with AI concepts such as NLP, computer vision, recommender systems, and conversational AI.  
  * Awareness of Generative AI applications (LLMs, prompt engineering, content creation, AI copilots).

* **Analytical Skills:** Strong ability to analyze business workflows, identify gaps, and propose improvements.

* **Technical Acumen:** Basic understanding of data pipelines, APIs, and AI model lifecycle.

* **Tools:** Proficiency in tools like JIRA, Confluence, Tableau/PowerBI, and knowledge of Python/SQL (good-to-have).

* **Soft Skills:** Strong communication, stakeholder management, and documentation skills.

**Preferred (Nice-to-Have)**

* Exposure to **cloud platforms** (AWS, GCP, Azure) with AI/ML services.  
* Understanding of **MLOps and AI governance**.  
* Experience in building or deploying **chatbots, copilots, or GenAI-powered assistants**.
