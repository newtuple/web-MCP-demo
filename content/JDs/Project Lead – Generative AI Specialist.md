**About the Role**  
We're seeking a **Project Manager – Generative AI Specialist** who lives and breathes AI. This role is perfect for someone who leverages tools like ChatGPT, Claude, or similar daily to enhance their work, streamline workflows, and create innovative solutions. You’ll lead cutting-edge GenAI projects, collaborating with diverse teams to deliver impactful results. Your expertise in prompt engineering, workflow optimization, and running GenAI projects will make you a vital contributor to our mission of transforming businesses through AI.

  - Take end-to-end ownership of Generative AI projects, ensuring successful delivery aligned with business goals.

* **Collaboration & Business Analysis**  
    
  - Work with clients, stakeholders, and technical teams to gather, validate, and document requirements.

  - Conduct market research and competitive analysis to inform product strategy and roadmap development.


* **Innovation & Optimization**  
    
  - Continuously explore, experiment, and recommend AI tools, frameworks, and methodologies to optimize project workflows.  
  - Define and prioritize product features, aligning them with business value and customer feedback.


* **Communication & Documentation**  
    
  - Prepare and deliver project documentation, including requirements, specifications, and status updates.
