Two and a half years ago I founded Newtuple because I couldn’t shake the feeling that Generative AI would change how the whole world builds software. That hunch paid off.

Since then, Newtuple's scrappy, caffeine‑fueled, awesome team has shipped twenty‑five plus GenAI projects, many of which are in production. We’ve powered voice‑based interview copilots, multi‑agent legal assistants, deep domain document extractors for financial services, and even an AI agent that helps frontline staff at children's home do a better job of providing care.

Those victories felt great, but every project surfaced the same headaches:

*   “Will this MVP survive real traffic when we scale workloads?”

*   "Do I need to rebuild everything from scratch when we go-live?"

*   “What happens when this vendor doubles prices or changes their API tomorrow?”

*   “Who’s going to babysit the pipeline at two in the morning?”

> **Sprint after sprint, we carved out reusable chunks of code. Eventually those chunks snapped together into something bigger. Today we finally get to share those building blocks with you. We call them** [**Accelerators**](/genai-accelerators)**, and they are the shortcuts I wish we’d had on day one.**

## Everyone is chasing speed: don’t lose the forest for the trees

Every team I talk to wants to ship GenAI faster. Velocity without guard‑rails can turn into spaghetti code, runaway costs, and three am frantic calls from your users. We’ve taken that journey multiple times, sometimes the hard way, and we have boiled the lessons into these accelerators so you can skip the bruises and keep your eyes on the bigger prize: sustainable, scalable impact.

So here's what we've already thought through:

*   **Security guard‑rails baked in.** Role‑based access, audit logs, rate limiting, your CISO can sleep at night.

*   **Swap‑in or swap‑out components.** LLMs, embeddings, vector stores, databases, all hot‑pluggable so you can build on top of your favourite stack.

*   **From quick demo to production, no rewrite, no drama.** One code path from day one, so scaling feels like turning a dial, not rebuilding a house.

*   **Runs anywhere, AWS, Azure, GCP.** Choose your flavour and change your mind later without tears.

*   **Heartbeat monitoring.** Dashboards, alerts, and regression tests that chirp before your users do.

If that list reads like a catalogue of founder nightmares, that is because it is. We bottled them up so you can focus on what makes your product unique.

## Why not use SaaS or Open Source?

Here's why:

*   **SaaS** feels magical until prices creep up, the roadmap drifts, and your exec team asks why you can’t migrate.

*   **Open source** feels liberating until you are spelunking through a twelve‑month‑old pull request at midnight, wondering why your build suddenly broke.

Our accelerators aim for the middle path: fast like SaaS, flexible like open source, without the lock‑in or maintenance nightmares. **We provide the entire codebase to our clients,** so your team can modify and extend it exactly the way you need. We are dedicated to building alongside you, which means that we will actively collaborate with your team throughout the development process. This partnership approach ensures that we can address any challenges or questions that may arise in real-time, providing the necessary support and expertise to facilitate a smooth implementation. You will never have to worry about the complexities of maintenance sinkholes, as our team is there to guide you, helping to navigate any potential pitfalls and ensuring that your software remains robust and scalable over time.
