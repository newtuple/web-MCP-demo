# B2B Sales Agency: 10x Performance Gains with Zero-Dollar Data Stack

**GenAI-Powered Data Intelligence**

## Card Summary
Built a comprehensive data analytics platform for a B2B sales agency managing 50+ clients, reducing incentive calculation time from 15 days to under 1 day and achieving 90% reduction in data quality issues using open-source tools.

---

## The Challenge

A rapidly growing B2B sales agency managing over 50 diverse clients faced severe operational challenges stemming from fragmented data and manual processes. Their sales operations team struggled with unreliable reporting, inefficient lead management, and error-prone incentive calculations that were undermining team morale and business performance.

**Key Problems:**
- **Data Silos:** Performance metrics dispersed across Apollo, PeopleHR, and Hubspot with no unified view
- **Manual Incentive Calculations:** 15+ days per cycle using complex Excel spreadsheets, leading to frequent errors
- **High SDR Turnover:** Inaccurate incentive calculations caused low satisfaction and retention issues
- **Poor Data Quality:** 90%+ error rate in reporting metrics across subdivisions
- **Fragmented Reporting:** Each client and subdivision maintained separate tracking systems
- **Lead Management Inefficiency:** High volume and complexity overwhelmed manual processes

The agency needed a scalable data infrastructure that could unify disparate sources, automate complex calculations, and provide reliable insights - without breaking the bank.

---

Newtuple delivered a complete data transformation by building a "Zero Dollar Data Stack" from scratch using best-in-class open-source tools. The solution created an end-to-end data pipeline that automated data extraction, transformation, and visualization while enabling natural language queries through a GenAI interface.
