# Affordable Housing: AI-Powered Maintenance Management Platform

**Voice AI + Intelligent Routing**

## Card Summary
Built an AI-powered maintenance management platform for affordable housing, featuring voice agent call handling, intelligent work order routing, and automated compliance tracking to transform manual, fragmented workflows into streamlined digital operations.

---

## The Challenge

An affordable housing organization managing 14 buildings and approximately 600 apartments faced operational chaos stemming from entirely manual maintenance workflows. Their property management team struggled with fragmented systems, limited visibility, and compliance risks.

**Key Problems:**
- **Manual intake chaos:** Maintenance requests via voicemail, spreadsheets, and whiteboards
- **Zero visibility:** No centralized view of service requests or work order status
- **Inefficient vendor management:** Vendor assignments, compliance tracking, and performance monitoring done manually
- **Legal risk:** Lack of auditability for habitability compliance and tenant service
- **Tenant dissatisfaction:** Slow response times and poor communication
- **Operational inefficiency:** Property managers spending excessive time on coordination vs. strategic work
- **Compliance gaps:** Missing documentation for insurance, regulatory requirements

The organization needed an intelligent system that could automate intake, streamline workflows, and provide transparency while meeting legal and compliance requirements.

---

## The Solution

Newtuple designed and delivered an AI-powered maintenance management platform with voice agent capabilities, intelligent routing, and comprehensive compliance features.

**AI-Powered Tenant Intake:**

**Voice Agent for Maintenance Calls:**
- Conversational AI handling inbound maintenance request calls
- Natural language understanding of issue descriptions
- Diagnostic prompting to gather complete information (location, urgency, symptoms)
- Automated work order creation from voice conversations
- Multilingual support (English/Spanish) for diverse tenant population
- Alternative web-based form intake for digital-first tenants

**Intelligent Issue Classification:**
- NLP-based categorization (plumbing, electrical, HVAC, pest control, etc.)
- Urgency scoring based on tenant description and keywords
- Automated priority assignment (emergency, high, medium, low)
- Pattern recognition for recurring issues at specific properties
- Sentiment analysis detecting tenant frustration or escalation needs

**Smart Work Order Management:**

**Automated Routing & Assignment:**
- Intelligent vendor matching based on specialization and availability
- Property-level vendor assignment preferences
- Workload balancing across maintenance teams
- Automated escalation for overdue or high-priority requests
- Manager approval workflows for high-cost repairs

**Vendor Management Intelligence:**
- Automated vendor onboarding with document collection (insurance, W-9)
- Specialization tagging (plumbing, electrical, pest control, painting, etc.)
- Performance tracking and quality scoring
- Compliance monitoring (insurance expiration alerts)
- Workload visibility preventing vendor overload

**Compliance & Legal Features:**

**Audit Trail Automation:**
- Complete timestamped history of every work order
- Photo and document attachment capabilities
- Tenant communication logs
- Completion verification with tenant signatures
- Service history summaries for legal defense

**Tenant Satisfaction Tracking:**
- Automated post-completion surveys (1-5 rating)
- Negative feedback escalation to management
- Satisfaction trends by property, vendor, and issue type
- Dispute documentation and resolution tracking

**Response Time Analytics:**
- SLA tracking (time to acknowledge, time to complete)
- Performance dashboards by property and vendor
- Habitability compliance monitoring
- Audit-ready reports for regulatory reviews

**Operational Intelligence:**

**Real-Time Dashboard:**
- Open vs. closed work orders visualization
- Aging analysis for overdue requests
- Vendor performance and workload metrics
- Property-level issue trends
- Cost tracking and budget variance

**Predictive Insights:**
- Recurring issue detection suggesting preventive maintenance
- Seasonal pattern recognition for proactive preparation
- Property-level issue clustering identifying systemic problems
- Vendor performance trends for contract renewal decisions
